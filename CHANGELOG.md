# Changelog

Versions follow [semantic versioning](https://semver.org). Before 1.0, a minor
version may change what a host has to do; each section says so under
**Upgrading**.

## 0.1.0 (2026-11-19)

The first release. Hushlist has run one family's wishlists since November
2026; this is its first version for anyone else.

- One list per person, a secret family link for guests, and the one rule:
  nobody sees what is reserved on their own list.
- Paste a link and the wish fills itself in. Your own pictures, hide from
  others, sizes, "Got it" with a history by occasion, print.
- English and Danish, chosen by the household (`docs/TRANSLATING.md` for more).
- Wish from anywhere: a shared iPhone Shortcut by iCloud link, a bookmark for
  computers, or paste a whole list.
- A nightly check of every wish's link.
- The image: `ghcr.io/rcapion/hushlist:0.1.0`, for amd64 and arm64.

**Upgrading:** nothing to upgrade from. Set up as in `AGENTS.md`.
